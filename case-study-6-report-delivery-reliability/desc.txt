
CASE STUDY 6
MERIDIAN PAYMENTS
Generates scheduled financial statements for business customers. A job runner logs every attempt.
"Every so often a tenant’s scheduled report just… doesn’t show up, and we only find out when they email asking where it is. Our job runner logs every attempt with a status, but nobody’s proactively watching it.
I want something that looks at our job history, works out which report runs never succeeded, and ideally retries the ones that are safe to retry, before a customer notices. What counts as ‘failed’ isn’t consistent in our logs — part of the mess I’m hoping you can untangle."
— Sam Okafor, Head of Platform Reliability
THE ASK IS DELIBERATELY
You decide — and document — which values really mean failure, how many retries reasonable before escalating to a human, the approach changes as tenants and grow.
WHAT WE'RE EVALUATING
•
Handling ambiguity
•
Soundness of design judgment
•
Building under real constraints
•
Clarity of your reasoning
•
Response when challenged
Provided: synthetic job-attempt logs across 6 and 12 report runs, plus a starter repo.
